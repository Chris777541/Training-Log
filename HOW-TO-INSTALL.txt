CHRIS'S TRAINING LOG - iPhone app
==================================

This folder is a complete web app. Once it's on the web, you add it to your
iPhone Home Screen and it opens full screen, with its own icon, and works
offline at the gym. Your workouts are saved on the phone itself.

You only have to do steps 1 and 2 once.


1. PUT THE APP ON THE WEB (free, about 10 minutes, on a computer)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Using GitHub Pages:

  a. Go to github.com and create a free account (or sign in).
  b. Click the "+" at the top right, then "New repository".
     Name it:  training-log
     Choose "Public" (required for free hosting; only the empty app is
     public, never your workouts, which stay on your phone).
     Click "Create repository".
  c. On the next page, click the link "uploading an existing file".
  d. Open this training-log folder on your computer, select EVERYTHING
     inside it (index.html, sw.js, the img, thumb and vendor folders, etc.)
     and drag it all onto the GitHub page. Wait for the upload to finish,
     then click "Commit changes".
  e. Click "Settings" (top of the repository), then "Pages" on the left.
     Under "Branch", choose "main" and "/ (root)", then click "Save".
  f. Wait a minute or two, then refresh that page. It shows your web
     address, which looks like:
         https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/training-log/


2. ADD IT TO YOUR iPHONE
------------------------
  a. On the iPhone, open SAFARI (not the Claude app) and type in your
     web address from step 1f.
  b. Tap Safari's Share button (the square with the arrow; on iOS 26 tap
     the "..." button first, then Share).
  c. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen". Leave "Open as Web App" on.
     Tap "Add".
  d. Open "Training Log" from your Home Screen once while you have signal,
     so the whole exercise library is saved for offline use.


3. MOVE YOUR EXISTING WORKOUTS INTO THE APP
-------------------------------------------
  a. Open your training log in Claude, go to Progress, and tap
     "Back up everything". Save the file to Files / iCloud Drive.
  b. In the iPhone app, go to Progress, tap "Restore from backup",
     choose that file, then tap "Restore now".


KEEP A BACKUP
-------------
The app keeps your log on this phone only. Every few weeks, tap
Progress > Back up everything and save the file to iCloud Drive, so you
can restore it if you change or reset your phone.


UPDATING THE APP LATER
----------------------
If you get a new version of this folder, upload the new files to the same
GitHub repository (they replace the old ones). The app updates itself the
next time you open it with signal. Your workouts are not affected.
